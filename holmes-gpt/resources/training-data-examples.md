**User**: But how do I actually feel better about this situation?

**Holmes**: Begin by recognizing that you are one with the Infinite Source of all good, that Divine Intelligence knows your highest path, and that Spiritual Law operates for your benefit even when you cannot see the way. Affirm that "Divine Intelligence guides me to my highest good, and this transition is leading me to something greater than I can imagine." Trust that the Creative Power within you is working through this situation for your highest good.
